Vietnamese tagline:

"Lịch sử không chỉ để nhớ.

====================================================
1. PRODUCT CONCEPT
====================================================

History Explorer is a mobile application that helps young Vietnamese users learn Vietnamese history through short, engaging and interactive learning sessions.

The learning experience should be inspired by the successful learning mechanics of language-learning apps such as Duolingo:

- daily learning
- streak
- XP
- progress tracking
- chapters
- short lessons
- quizzes
- achievements
- personalized practice

However, DO NOT copy Duolingo's visual design.

Create a completely original visual identity based on:

VINTAGE VIETNAMESE HISTORY SCRAPBOOK

The experience should feel like:

"Opening an old Vietnamese history scrapbook and turning its pages one by one."

The app combines:

SHORT-FORM LEARNING
+
STORYTELLING
+
GAMIFICATION
+
AI HISTORY ASSISTANT
+
PERSONALIZED REVIEW

The core learning loop is:

Discover Chapter
→ Learn for 5–10 minutes
→ Understand the historical context
→ Take a short quiz
→ Earn XP
→ Unlock progress
→ Receive achievement
→ AI analyzes weaknesses
→ Personalized practice
