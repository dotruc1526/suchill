# Storyboard · Tập 01 — Trước cơn bão

## Chỉ dẫn bố cục áp dụng cho toàn bộ 11 sequence

Recompose every shot natively for vertical mobile 9:16 (1080×1920). Do not zoom, crop, stretch, or enlarge any existing horizontal footage. Do not place a landscape video inside the vertical frame. Rebuild each scene as a true vertical composition using layered historical elements, background extension, foreground cut-outs, maps, documents, and SỬu positioned naturally from top to bottom. Keep the main subject fully visible inside the center safe area. Use the extra upper and lower space for environment, paper textures, maps, props, depth layers, or animated background—not for large subtitle boxes.

Subtitles must be small overlays near the bottom and use no more than 10–12% of the screen height. Never create a split layout, top video box, bottom caption panel, black bars, blurred duplicate background, or oversized text area.

Transitions should feel continuous and cinematic: use object-based match cuts, camera pan, parallax, foreground wipe, page turn, map zoom, and motion carried from one shot into the next. Avoid abrupt scene replacement.

The final result must look like it was originally designed and animated in 9:16, not converted from 16:9.

**Negative constraint:** DO NOT: crop 16:9 footage into 9:16, zoom horizontal scenes, stretch images, duplicate or blur the same image as background, use black bars, place visuals only in the upper half, create a large subtitle panel, or leave empty space around the main visual. Also avoid modern clothing, smartphones, futuristic technology, neon colors, graphic violence, gore, distorted hands, extra limbs, unreadable text, glossy 3D cartoons, and modern cityscapes.

**Nhịp dựng:** Không chuyển cảnh theo từng câu thoại. Mỗi sequence giữ một bối cảnh chính trong 6–12 giây và phát triển hình ảnh liên tục bên trong.
