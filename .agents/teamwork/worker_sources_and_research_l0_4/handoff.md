## 2. Logic Chain
- *Step 1 (From Observation 1):* Trần Quý Cáp street in District 3 was renamed to Võ Văn Tần, while Phan Đình Phùng became Nguyễn Đình Chiểu. Official Relic Decision 1288-VH/QĐ designates the site as the weapon bunker for the assault on Independence Palace (Dinh Độc Lập) by Đội 5 Biệt động. Assigning it to Đội 11 (US Embassy) and claiming it is now on Nguyễn Đình Chiểu is factually false and directly contradicts the project's own Screenplay, Curriculum Map, and Quiz Question 3.
- *Step 2 (From Observation 2):* The inclusion of "Biệt khu Thủ đô" instead of "Bộ Tư lệnh Hải quân" as the 5th target in `HISTORICAL-SOURCES.md` breaks the canonical data contract with `PILOT-SCREENPLAY.md` and `CURRICULUM-MAP.md`, where the 5 shock targets are firmly defined.
- *Step 3 (From Observation 3):* The timezone difference (+1 hour between GMT+7 and GMT+8) only created a 24-hour difference in Tet celebration because the astronomical New Moon (Sóc) for the turn of the year crossed midnight (23:29 GMT+7 on 29/01/1968 vs 00:29 GMT+8 on 30/01/1968), making Tháng Chạp in the North a 29-day month (Tet on Jan 30) and in the South a 30-day month (Tet on Jan 31). An oversimplified explanation in the sources document directly spawned the historical blunder in `CURRICULUM-MAP.md` line 143 where Month 11 was wrongly cited.
- *Step 4 (From Observation 4):* AP and British Pathé are commercial entities whose YouTube videos are protected by international copyright. Neither Vietnamese IP Law (Article 25) nor US Fair Use (17 U.S.C. § 107) allows ripping and hosting their proprietary video files in an EdTech app's server or distribution package. A compliant strategy requires public domain US federal works (NARA under 17 U.S.C. § 105), domestic state partnerships (VTV, TTXVN), or official YouTube API embeds.
- *Step 5 (From Observation 5 & 6):* Relying on `thuvienphapluat.vn` and `vietnamhoinhap.vn` while omitting the foundational works of Viện Lịch sử Quân sự Việt Nam severely undermines the academic legitimacy of the project. Institutional attributions must accurately reflect the non-public status of Bảo tàng Biệt động Sài Gòn - Gia Định alongside state relic archives.
