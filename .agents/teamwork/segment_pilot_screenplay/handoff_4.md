### 3. [Major] WebVTT Timestamp Format Inconsistency Threatening Video Player Sync
- **Location**: Section 4 (Lines 99–158).
- **Discrepancy / Evaluation**:
  - In lines 99–136, timestamps strictly follow the 3-part canonical syntax `hh:mm:ss.ttt` (e.g. `00:00:01.000 --> 00:00:06.500`, `00:01:07.000 --> 00:01:10.500`).

- **Remediation**:
  - Standardize all cues from 01:10 onwards to uniform 3-part timestamps:
    * `00:01:10.800 --> 00:01:14.000`
    * `00:01:14.200 --> 00:01:17.800`
    * `00:01:18.000 --> 00:01:25.000`
    * `00:01:25.500 --> 00:01:32.000`
    * `00:01:32.500 --> 00:01:40.000`
    * `00:01:40.500 --> 00:01:45.500`

### 4. [Major] Unexplained Calendar Shift Between Northern (Hanoi) and Southern (Saigon) Lunar Dates
- **Location**: Section 2 (Lines 41, 43), Section 5 (Lines 176, 181).
- **Discrepancy / Evaluation**:
  - The script oscillates between calling the attack time *"Đêm Giao thừa... đêm 30 Tết"* (Scene 01, Line 41) and *"rạng sáng Mùng 2 Tết"* (Scene 03 Visual Cue Line 43; Fallback Card Line 181: *"Rạng sáng Mùng 2 Tết (31/1/1968)"*).
  - In 1968, North Vietnam used GMT+7 (Hanoi) while the Republic of Vietnam administration enforced GMT+8 (Saigon) from 1960. Due to new-moon calculation across the midnight boundary, the lunar month differed by one day: in Saigon, the night of January 30 entering January 31 was Giao thừa (Mùng 1 Tết), whereas in Hanoi, Giao thừa was the night of January 29 entering January 30 (making January 31 Mùng 2 Tết).
  - While Scene 03 visual cue includes a graphic map comparing GMT+7 and GMT+8, neither the voiceover nor captions provide any verbal explanation. To a modern student, hearing "Đêm 30 Tết Giao thừa" in Scene 01 and then reading "Rạng sáng Mùng 2 Tết (31/1)" in Scene 03 and Fallback Card appears as a glaring factual contradiction.
- **Remediation**:
  - Harmonize the narrative by explicitly stating the historical context in the Fallback Card and WebVTT: *"Rạng sáng ngày 31/1/1968 (tức đêm Giao thừa Tết miền Nam, tương ứng rạng sáng Mùng 2 Tết theo lịch miền Bắc)"*.
